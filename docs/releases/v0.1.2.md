# OpenCam v0.1.2

## 繁體中文

- 改善中斷救援，支援分段 MKV 與舊版單一工作檔。
- 工作階段資訊遺失或損壞時，可由現存錄影片段重建並嘗試救援。
- 空白或損壞尾段不再阻擋可讀內容的救援，並明確顯示完整或部分救回。
- 改善錄影、暫停、停止及 MP4 封裝期間的狀態更新；救援時保留原始 MKV。

## English

- Improved interruption recovery for segmented MKVs and legacy single-file recordings.
- Reconstruct recovery information from surviving recordings when session metadata is missing or damaged.
- Recover readable content despite empty or damaged trailing segments, with clear full/partial recovery results.
- Improved status updates during recording, pause, stop and MP4 packaging, while preserving original MKVs during recovery.
