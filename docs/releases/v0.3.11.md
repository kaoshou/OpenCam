# OpenCam v0.3.11

## 繁體中文

- 錄製前檢查改為只確認麥克風與系統聲音，移除錄影範圍預覽。
- 五秒試錄改為純音訊測試，完成後可回放，不加入正式專案。
- 音源卡片下方新增獨立狀態區，顯示載入、啟動試錄、收音、準備回放與正式錄製準備。
- 只開啟已選音源；實際啟動後才計時，正式錄影前先釋放試錄裝置。
- 關閉檢查或未選音源時仍顯示錄製準備；正式影片設定不變。

## English

- Preflight now checks microphone/system audio only, removing the capture-area preview.
- The five-second trial records audio for replay without adding it to the project.
- A separate area below the audio cards shows loading, trial startup, capture, replay preparation and formal recording preparation.
- Only selected inputs are opened. Timing starts after capture begins, and trial devices are released before formal recording.
- Preparation remains visible with checks disabled or no inputs selected; formal video settings are unchanged.
