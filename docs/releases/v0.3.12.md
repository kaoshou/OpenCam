# OpenCam v0.3.12

## 繁體中文

- 原本影片與編輯視窗的「輸出…」在 MP4 下方新增 GIF，並保留網頁播放器選項。
- 片段清單與時間軸右鍵新增「匯出片段…」子選單，滑鼠移入後可選 MP4 或 GIF。匯出保留裁切範圍；合併片段包含全部子段，MP4 保留適用的音訊與效果。
- GIF 提供 480／640／960／原始大小與 10／15／20 FPS，預設為 640／15 FPS；保持比例、不放大並無限循環。
- GIF 沒有音訊，也不限制影片長度。匯出長度達五分鐘時，會提醒轉換時間、檔案大小及無音訊，確認後仍可繼續。
- 錄製前檢查改為確認聲音，僅開啟已選音源；五秒試錄改為聲音測試，完成後可回放。
- 音源卡片下方新增獨立狀態區，顯示載入、啟動試錄、試錄中、處理試錄、回放與正式錄製準備；實際啟動後才開始五秒計時。
- 開始正式錄影前會先釋放試錄裝置。正式影片設定不變，繁體中文與英文介面同步更新。

## English

- Added GIF below MP4 in the original video's Export menu and the editor, while retaining Web player export.
- Added an **Export clip…** submenu to clip-list and timeline context menus. Hover to choose MP4 or GIF. Exports respect retained trims and include all parts of a compound clip; MP4 retains applicable audio and effects.
- GIF offers 480/640/960/original width and 10/15/20 FPS, defaulting to 640/15 FPS. It preserves aspect ratio without upscaling and loops indefinitely.
- GIF has no audio and no duration limit. Exports of five minutes or longer show a confirmation about conversion time, file size and missing audio, with the option to continue.
- Recording preflight now checks sound using only the selected audio inputs. The five-second trial records audio and supports replay after completion.
- A separate status area below the audio cards shows loading, trial startup, recording, finalizing, replay and preparation for formal recording. The five-second timer starts after capture actually begins.
- Trial devices are released before formal recording starts. Formal video settings are unchanged, and Traditional Chinese and English interfaces are updated together.
