## 繁體中文

- 原本影片與編輯視窗的「輸出…」在 MP4 下方新增 GIF，保留網頁播放器選項。
- 片段清單與時間軸右鍵的「匯出片段…」為滑鼠移入展開的 MP4／GIF 子選單。只匯出指定邏輯片段的保留範圍；合併片段含全部子段，不擴展至其他群組成員。MP4 保留適用的音訊與效果。
- GIF 明示沒有音訊，提供 480／640／960／原始大小與 10／15／20 FPS（預設 640／15），保持比例、不放大、無限循環。沒有長度上限；達五分鐘需再次確認耗時、檔案大小與無音訊後才繼續。
- GIF 先將畫面暫存到磁碟，再產生色盤與動畫；完成後校驗影格、尺寸及時間，不把整段畫面長期堆在記憶體。保留來源身分、版本檢查、取消與不覆蓋發布保護；不修改原始素材或專案。明確格式／範圍匯出失敗後需重新從選單選取，避免舊重試命令誤匯出整部 MP4。
- 本版也包含先前 0.3.11 原始碼的純音訊錄前檢查：只開已選音源，不擷取預覽畫面；五秒試錄收到實際啟動確認才計時，封存驗證後可聲音回放。音源卡片下獨立狀態區顯示載入、試錄與正式錄製準備。確認後先釋放試錄裝置再開始正式錄影；結果不明時不冒充成功。正式影片設定不變。
- 繁體中文／英文同步。修正 headless 測試 teardown 時排程中的版面工作在服務釋放後執行的測試環境競態，不改正式程式字型或錯誤處理。

### 相容性與限制

- GIF 無音訊、色彩有限，畫質及播放節奏可能與 MP4 不同。長片、高解析度原始畫布需要大量暫存空間與處理時間；未承諾固定資源上限。Windows／macOS 原生滑鼠子選單、鍵盤、高 DPI、不同 GIF 播放器、長片資源與完整逐像素驗收仍需人工確認。
- 無新增應用依賴或專案格式。字幕仍格式4，0.3.3及以前不能開啟；請備份完整專案含重點附屬檔。字幕不燒入影片／GIF，不自動對時。
- 純音訊試錄不驗證畫面擷取與影片編碼器；回放後需重新檢查。原生四音源、裝置拔除、慢啟停、長 A/V、GPU、多螢幕／游標、實際磁碟耗盡、瀏覽器離線／觸控／全螢幕、浮窗排除及背景快捷鍵等完整實體 Release Gate 未全部完成。歷史 Windows 心跳／試錄／快速輸出偶發問題不宣稱根治。
- 套件未具可信發行者簽章或 macOS 公證；Windows FFmpeg 完整對應原始碼核對仍待完成。依使用者授權發布，不代表全部可靠性、安全、授權或 Stable／實機驗收通過。採作者自查，非獨立審查。舊示範圖保留原版本。

## English

- The original video's Export menu and the editor now offer GIF below MP4, retaining Web player export.
- Hover **Export clip…** in either the clip-list or timeline context menu to open its MP4/GIF submenu. Export includes only the retained range of that logical clip, including compound parts but not other grouped clips. MP4 retains applicable audio and effects.
- GIF explicitly has no audio. Choose 480/640/960/original width and 10/15/20 FPS (default 640/15), preserving aspect ratio without upscaling and looping indefinitely. No duration cap; five minutes or longer requires another confirmation about conversion time, size and missing audio.
- GIF uses disk-backed frame staging, palette generation and animation verification of frames, dimensions and timing rather than retaining the entire movie in memory. Source identity, revision checks, cancellation and non-overwriting publication remain. Originals and projects are unchanged. After explicit-format/target export failure, reselect from the menu instead of using the legacy retry command that could otherwise export a whole MP4.
- Includes the previously developed 0.3.11 audio-only preflight: selected inputs only, no screen preview; five-second timing starts after actual acknowledgement, and replay follows sealing/validation. A separate status area shows loading, trials and formal recording preparation. Trial devices are released before formal recording; unknown outcomes are not reported as success. Formal video settings remain unchanged.
- Traditional Chinese and English are updated together. A headless-test teardown race now drains queued layout work before its services are released; production fonts and error handling are unchanged.

### Compatibility and limits

- GIF has no audio and a limited palette; quality and playback pacing may differ from MP4. Long/high-resolution source canvases require substantial temporary disk space and processing time, with no fixed resource ceiling promised. Native Windows/macOS submenu hover, keyboard, high DPI, different GIF viewers, long-clip resources and complete pixel-reference acceptance still require manual validation.
- No new application dependency or project format. Subtitle projects retain format4, incompatible with 0.3.3 and earlier. Back up whole projects including bookmark sidecars. Captions are not burned into video/GIF or automatically retimed.
- Audio trials do not validate screen capture/video encoders; recheck after replay. The complete physical Release Gate remains incomplete for native four-source audio, unplugging, slow startup/shutdown, long A/V, GPU, multiple displays/cursor, physical disk exhaustion, browser offline/touch/fullscreen, capture exclusion and background shortcuts. Historical intermittent Windows heartbeat/trial/fast-export issues are not claimed resolved.
- No trusted publisher signature or macOS notarization; complete corresponding-source verification for Windows FFmpeg remains pending. Publication is user-authorized, not complete reliability, security, licensing, Stable or device acceptance. Author self-review only, not independent review. Existing illustrations retain their actual version labels.
