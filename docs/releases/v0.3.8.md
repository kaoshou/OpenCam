# OpenCam v0.3.8

## 繁體中文

- 修正錄前預覽在初始化或主動停止時可能被誤判為擷取失敗，造成持續黑畫面的問題。
- 整理錄前視窗、音量卡與按鈕，載入、試錄、完成及失敗狀態更清楚。
- 移除錄製倒數，確認後直接準備錄製，以主畫面「錄製中」狀態為準。
- 未選音源明確顯示本次不會錄製；試錄期間顯示所選來源的即時音量。
- 編輯器可在播放頭位置新增重點；網站截圖改用可關閉的頁內燈箱。

## English

- Fixed preflight initialization or intentional shutdown being mistaken for a capture failure and leaving a black preview.
- Refined the preflight window, audio cards and buttons, with clearer loading, trial, ready and failure states.
- Removed the recording countdown. Confirmation now starts preparation; the home screen indicates when recording begins.
- Unselected inputs explicitly say they will not be recorded; selected inputs show live levels during trials.
- Added bookmarks at the editor playhead and an inline, dismissible screenshot lightbox on the website.
