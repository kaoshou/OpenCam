# OpenCam v0.4.1

## 繁體中文

### 網頁播放器閱讀設定

- 新增右上角「閱讀設定」浮層，預設關閉；語言選擇與搜尋保留在外面，逐字稿填滿剩餘閱讀空間。
- 可調整逐字稿字級（100–200%）、行距、深色高對比／暖白背景、跟隨播放，並可一鍵重設。
- 設定浮層不擠壓逐字稿，可按關閉、Esc 或點外側關閉，支援手機與平板橫直向的內部捲動。

### 完整逐字稿下載

- 新增閱讀版 HTML 與純文字 TXT 下載，方便離線閱讀與列印。
- 下載包含所選語言的全部字幕文字，不受搜尋與清單分批載入影響；可選擇是否包含時間標記，閱讀版保留章節分段。

### 操作與版面改善

- 改善播放器鍵盤操作、焦點提示、跳至逐字稿、字幕設定關閉與焦點返回。
- 閱讀逐字稿時，跟隨播放不會搶走清單內的鍵盤焦點。
- 改善官方網站與中英文使用說明的鍵盤導覽、文字對比、窄螢幕換行與圖片載入排版。

## English

### Web player reading settings

- Added a top-right Reading settings overlay, closed by default. Language and search stay outside, while the transcript fills the remaining reading area.
- Adjust transcript text size (100–200%), spacing, dark contrast/warm backgrounds and follow playback, with a reset option.
- The overlay does not shrink the transcript. Close it with its close button, Escape or an outside click; its contents scroll on phone and tablet portrait/landscape layouts.

### Full transcript downloads

- Download standalone reading HTML or plain TXT for offline reading and printing.
- Downloads include every caption in the selected language, independent of search and paged list loading. Timestamps are optional, and the reading HTML retains chapter sections.

### Navigation and layout improvements

- Improved player keyboard operation, focus indicators, skip-to-transcript, and caption-menu dismissal with focus return.
- Follow playback does not take keyboard focus away while reading within the transcript list.
- Improved keyboard navigation, text contrast, narrow-screen wrapping and image-loading layout on the website and bilingual user guides.
