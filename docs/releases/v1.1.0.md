# OpenCam v1.1.0

## 螢幕註記

錄影時可在畫面上用畫筆、直線、箭頭、方框或圓圈標示重點，調整顏色和粗細。橡皮擦可按住左鍵拖曳，移除碰到的整筆註記；也可復原、重做或清除全部。

工具列可拖曳、收合或透過快捷鍵隱藏。畫筆、工具列及繪圖工具快捷鍵可在設定中調整；F10 預設切換畫筆，保留已有筆跡；繪圖畫面或工具列有焦點時，Esc 預設結束繪圖並清除註記。既有自訂快捷鍵會保留，請以設定畫面為準。

註記與工具列可能出現在錄影中，註記直接錄入影片，無法事後移除；筆跡固定於螢幕位置，不跟隨教材捲動。可先短錄測試，再開始正式錄製。

本版也改善主畫面教鞭樣式及大小的選擇介面，並修正工具列在跨螢幕移動及反覆隱藏時可能縮小的問題。

## Screen annotations

Annotate while recording using a pen, lines, arrows, rectangles or ellipses, with adjustable colors and line widths. Drag the eraser to remove whole marks, or undo, redo and clear them.

Move, collapse or hide the toolbar and customize drawing shortcuts in Settings. By default, F10 toggles drawing while keeping marks; Esc ends drawing and clears marks when the drawing surface or toolbar has focus. Existing shortcut preferences are preserved.

Annotations and the toolbar may appear in the recording. Marks are recorded into the video and cannot be removed later; they stay at screen positions when the material scrolls. Make a short test recording first.

This release also improves the teaching pointer selector and addresses toolbar size changes after moving between displays and repeatedly hiding it.
